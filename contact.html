Frequently asked

How long does shipping take?

In-stock orders leave our Athens warehouse within two business days and typically arrive in 3–6 days depending on your location. You'll get tracking as soon as it ships.

What's your return policy?

Anything unused within 30 days of delivery can be returned for a full refund. Seasoned cast iron and used textiles are final sale for hygiene reasons — we'll flag this on the product page.

Do you sell wholesale or to restaurants?

Yes — select "Wholesale & press" in the contact form above and our sourcing lead will follow up with volume pricing.

How do I care for cast iron or carbon steel?

Hand wash, dry immediately, and rub with a thin layer of neutral oil while still warm. Every cookware product page includes specific care notes under the Specifications tab.

Can I visit the warehouse?

We open the Athens warehouse for public visits on the first Saturday of every month — email us and we'll add you to the list.
