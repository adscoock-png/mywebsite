Home / Our Story

We started in a kitchen that was falling apart.

Literally — the linoleum was peeling and the only pan that worked was a warped skillet from a thrift store. That skillet outlasted three apartments and taught us more about cooking than anything we'd bought new. Fieldstone & Fern exists to sell more things like it.

What we believe

Three ideas that shape every buying decision we make.

Repair over replace

We favor materials that can be re-seasoned, re-oiled, or re-sharpened instead of thrown out at the first sign of wear.

Real people, real workshops

Every maker we carry is someone on our team has met in person — no anonymous factory listings, ever.

Fewer, better things

We'd rather sell you one cutting board you keep for fifteen years than five you replace every winter.

How we got here

2016

A wedding registry, mostly

Fieldstone & Fern began as a spreadsheet of the cookware and linens we'd actually recommend to friends getting married.

2019

First workshop visit

We drove to a small ironworks in Ohio to watch our first Dutch ovens being cast — and decided every future product had to earn a visit like that.

2022

Opened the Athens warehouse

Outgrew the garage. Our current warehouse ships nearly every order within two business days.

2026

12,000 kitchens and counting

Still a small team, still visiting workshops, still answering the support line ourselves.

The team

Six people, one warehouse, a lot of strong opinions about cast iron.

"We don't do drop-shipping. If we haven't held it, seasoned it, or eaten off it ourselves, it doesn't go up for sale."

— Founding team, Fieldstone & Fern
