How we choose what we sell

Three questions every product has to answer before it makes the catalogue.

01

Will it last a decade?

We look for materials and joinery that improve with age instead of wearing out — cast iron over coated aluminum, solid wood over veneer.

02

Who's actually making it?

We visit the workshop, meet the maker, and ask about the material sourcing before anything goes up for sale.

03

Would we replace it with itself?

If our own team wouldn't buy the same thing again, it doesn't stay in the shop.
